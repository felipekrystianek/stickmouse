Installing Couchpad
===================

1. Drag Couchpad to the Applications folder.
2. Open Couchpad from Applications.

If macOS says Couchpad "cannot be opened" or "could not be verified":
open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway"
next to Couchpad, then confirm. You only need to do this once.

3. Couchpad asks for Accessibility access so it can move the pointer.
   Click "Open Settings" and switch on Couchpad, then click "Restart Now".

Couchpad is free for 7 days. Buy a license for $5 from the Couchpad menu
(the controller icon in the menu bar) > Buy Couchpad.
