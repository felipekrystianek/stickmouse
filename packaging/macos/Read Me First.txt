Installing Stickmouse
===================

1. Drag Stickmouse to the Applications folder.
2. Open Stickmouse from Applications.

If macOS says Stickmouse "cannot be opened" or "could not be verified":
open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway"
next to Stickmouse, then confirm. You only need to do this once.

3. Stickmouse asks for Accessibility access so it can move the pointer.
   Click "Open Settings" and switch on Stickmouse, then click "Restart Now".

Stickmouse is free for 7 days. Buy a license for $5 from the Stickmouse menu
(the controller icon in the menu bar) > Buy Stickmouse.
